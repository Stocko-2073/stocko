## Finding: a stopped wheel drives itself (floating STEP input)

In 2 of 3 stops after `drive 0.1 0 8` at 1492 mA, wheel A started moving on its own
about a second after stopping and ran until EN was cut. Captures at 50 Hz are in
`tests/results/2026-09-30/step-noise-selfstep-1500ma.csv` (it happened) and
`step-noise-clean-1500ma.csv` (it didn't):

- The ramp-down was normal: commanded rate 0.0 and the commanded step integral
  frozen at 24606 from t=267817 onward, for the rest of the capture.
- From t≈268829 (1.0 s after the stop): twitches of a count or two every 200-300 ms.
- From t≈270154: growing jerks, then continuous forward motion at 0.2-0.76 turns/s
  (up to ~50 cm/s at the rim), faster than the drive command itself.
- t=270724: FAULT_SLIP latched on A (B stopped by FAULT_PEER). A kept running.
- t≈271292: `disable` raised EN and the wheel coasted to rest.
- In the first occurrence the driver itself reported CS_ACTUAL 26 with stst clear
  4 s after the stop, and the fault-time sample (DRV_STATUS 0x001A0000) says it was
  stepping at run current. Echo faults stayed 0 throughout.

Cause: with VACTUAL = 0 the TMC2209 "reacts to STEP input" (datasheet, VACTUAL).
The protoboard leaves STEP unconnected, and unlike DIR it has no internal pull-down,
so noise coupled from the motor wiring toggles it. DIR's internal pull-down is why
every false step went the same way. The switch to a 20 V pack this morning (larger
and faster phase-voltage edges) is the likely reason it appears now; it started as
the driver began reducing towards hold current. Only wheel A has shown it.

It cannot happen while driving (VACTUAL ≠ 0 ignores STEP), so it does not explain
the grass stalls. The slip detector noticed, but its response (command zero) is the
state in which the pin is obeyed; only EN stopped it.

**Fix (hardware, both modules): tie STEP to GND**, directly or through ~10 kΩ. The
datasheet also says to tie CLK to GND for the internal clock; check CLK-to-GND on the
module with a meter and add a wire if it is open. Until then, don't leave the robot
enabled at a standstill unattended. Stand checks that hold a wheel stopped and enabled
(stationary boost, hold-current timing, thermocouple runs, the weight-lift fixture) and
all yard runs wait for this fix.
