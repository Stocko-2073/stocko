## Finding: a stopped wheel drives itself (floating STEP input)

In 2 of 3 stops after `drive 0.1 0 8` at 1492 mA, wheel A started moving on its own
about a second after stopping and ran until EN was cut. Captures at 50 Hz are in
`tests/results/2026-09-30/step-noise-selfstep-1500ma.csv` (it happened) and
`step-noise-clean-1500ma.csv` (it didn't):

- The ramp-down was normal: commanded rate 0.0 and the commanded step integral
  frozen at 24606 from t=267817 onward, for the rest of the capture.
- From t≈268829 (1.0 s after the stop): twitches of a count or two every 200-300 ms.
- From t≈270154: growing jerks, then continuous forward motion at 0.2-0.76 turns/s
  (up to ~50 cm/s at the rim), faster than the drive command itself.
- t=270724: FAULT_SLIP latched on A (B stopped by FAULT_PEER). A kept running.
- t≈271292: `disable` raised EN and the wheel coasted to rest.
- In the first occurrence the driver itself reported CS_ACTUAL 26 with stst clear
  4 s after the stop, and the fault-time sample (DRV_STATUS 0x001A0000) says it was
  stepping at run current. Echo faults stayed 0 throughout.

Cause: with VACTUAL = 0 the TMC2209 "reacts to STEP input" (datasheet, VACTUAL).
The protoboard leaves STEP unconnected, and unlike DIR it has no internal pull-down,
so noise coupled from the motor wiring toggles it. DIR's internal pull-down is why
every false step went the same way. The switch to a 20 V pack this morning (larger
and faster phase-voltage edges) is the likely reason it appears now; it started as
the driver began reducing towards hold current. Only wheel A has shown it.

It cannot happen while driving (VACTUAL ≠ 0 ignores STEP), so it does not explain
the grass stalls. The slip detector noticed, but its response (command zero) is the
state in which the pin is obeyed; only EN stopped it.

**Fix (hardware, both modules): tie STEP to GND**, directly or through ~10 kΩ. The
datasheet also says to tie CLK to GND for the internal clock; check CLK-to-GND on the
module with a meter and add a wire if it is open. Until then, don't leave the robot
enabled at a standstill unattended. Stand checks that hold a wheel stopped and enabled
(stationary boost, hold-current timing, thermocouple runs, the weight-lift fixture) and
all yard runs wait for this fix.

## After the fix: STEP and DIR tied to GND on both modules

The owner tied STEP and DIR to GND on both driver modules. Same 0.2.9 release build,
same scenario as the failing runs (`run_ma 1500`, `drive 0.1 0 8`, `wheel A/B reg 6F`
reads during and after the move), then 6-7.6 s of standstill with the drivers enabled
and a 50 Hz capture, repeated four times:

| run | A moved | B moved | drivers after the stop | faults |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.8 counts | 0.0 counts | standstill at +0.3/1.5/3/5 s | none |
| 2 | 1.2 | 2.0 | standstill throughout | none |
| 3 | 0.0 | 1.2 | standstill throughout | none |
| 4 | 1.2 | 0.8 | standstill throughout | none |

Movement is encoder settling (max |vel| 0.017 turns/s, the filter's noise floor). Hold
current stepped down 26 → 19 → 10 → 9 as IHOLDDELAY 8 paces it (~3.5 s, slower than one
step per 100 ms). Before the fix the same scenario self-stepped in 2 of 3 runs.

## 0.3.0 stand validation (live current, boost, thermal model, OTPW derate)

Bench build first (fault injection), then the release build, which is what stays
installed. `run_ma 1200` (base 1160/552 mA), `boost_ma 1768`, `boost_start_ms 1500`,
`therm_tau_s 600`, `therm_lim 1.10`. Captures:
`tests/results/2026-09-30/boost-start-manual-brace-0.3.0.csv` (50 Hz) and
`otpw-derate-injected-0.3.0.csv` (5 Hz).

**Boost, confirmed by each driver's CS_ACTUAL:**
- Start: the first move after a standstill got 1768/1768 mA for exactly 1.5 s (CS 31
  at +0.6 s); a second start after a pause got a fresh one.
- Manual: `boost on 5000` mid-drive took CS from 20 to 31 and held it 5.0 s.
- Stationary brace: `boost on 3000` while stopped gave CS 31 at standstill, hold
  included.
- Release: one current step (55 mA) per ~100 ms on run and hold together. Run is back
  at base after 1.1 s; hold reaches 552 mA 1.1 s later. At standstill the driver's own
  IHOLDDELAY pacing finished the hold ramp (CS 9 by +8 s).
- Wheel B's writes land one tick after A's: one live write per tick.
- 0 CS_ACTUAL mismatches throughout.

**Timing soak:** 60 s of `drive 0.2 0 60` with `boost on 500` every second.
- 54 requests, 528 live writes, 0 retries, 0 failures, 0 echo faults, 0 mismatches.
- Tick p50 <= 1.5 ms, but p99 in the 2.6-5.1 ms bucket and max 7.3 ms (goal was
  worst <= 4.5 ms).
- Single UART transactions reached ~2.5 ms against a ~0.7 ms estimate, and slot groups
  4.3 ms; 23 groups were put off by the tick budget.
- The hardware-I2C encoder (A, p50 <= 0.64 ms) was slower than the bit-banged one (B,
  p50 <= 0.38 ms).
- Follow up in Phase 3a (task pinning, bus timing) before tightening the loop.

**A real OTPW on both drivers during the soak.** Each driver logged one pre-warning
episode (120 C). Derate held at level 1 (boost blocked) and restored after 30 s clear
("driver pre-warning clear 30 s"), with no fault and EN kept low. At 1768 mA bursts on
20 V the driver chips, not the motors, are the boost ceiling: consider heatsinks or
airflow on the modules before relying on long boosts, or a lower `boost_ma`.

**Injected OTPW (`debug otpw A 40`, wheel A at 0.2 turns/s):**

| time | level | wheel A run / hold |
|---|---|---|
| 0 s | 1 (boost blocked) | 1160 / 552 mA |
| 10 s | 2 | 829 / 387 mA |
| 20 s | 3 | 552 / 276 mA |
| injection ends at 40 s; back down one level per 30 s clear | 2 at 70 s, 1 at 100 s, 0 at 130 s | |

Wheel B stayed at 1160 mA throughout, wheel A kept turning at every level, and the
timed job finished cleanly.
