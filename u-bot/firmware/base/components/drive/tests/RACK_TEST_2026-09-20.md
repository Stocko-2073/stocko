# Digital current control rack validation

Hardware confirmed by owner: 17HS15-1504S-X1 motors, BTT TMC2209 V1.3,
robot secured indoors with drive wheels clear, VMot powered, USB connected.
Final firmware: 0.1.5. Original application backed up locally before flashing
(`/tmp/ubot-before-digital-current.bin`); application slot only was flashed.

## Automated checks

- `python3 base/components/drive/tests/run_host_tests.py`: passed. Exercises
  nominal current quantization/limits, IFCNT wraparound and rejected writes,
  readback failures, simulated driver reset, SPREAD pin polarity, microstep and
  clock-gain threshold conversion, and refusal to enable on driver faults.
- ESP-IDF v6.1 build passed. Application 1,432,400 bytes; 27% OTA slot free.
- `git diff --check` passed for the changed firmware source and documentation.

## Hardware checks

- Boots disabled; current profile persisted and survived a software reboot.
- Requested 1200/600 mA RMS programs nominal 1160/552 mA (IRUN=20, IHOLD=9).
- Rejected `run_ma=1501`, hold current greater than run current, `iscale=1`,
  nonfinite current input, and changing current while enabled.
- Both wheels ran forward/reverse at 0.1 output turns/s for three seconds.
- Both ran together at 0.15 m/s; final 0.1.5 test ran for ten seconds.
- Running status: SpreadCycle, CS_ACTUAL=20, GSTAT=0 on both drivers.
- Timed commands stopped through the deadman. No motion/driver faults and no
  UART echo faults. Final ten-second travel: A +2.2119, B -2.2097 turns.
- Following separation of encoder and driver health polling, the final run's
  worst recorded tick was 5048 us against the nominal 5000 us period, including
  console activity. This is not a hard real-time deadline guarantee.
- Both motors were disabled after each sequence. Wheel B still reports a weak
  magnet (AGC=128); free-spin success does not resolve that mechanical issue.

## Power observation and remaining validation

The owner measured **13.2 V idle and a minimum 11.2 V at the main VMot rail**
during the ten-second run, recovering afterward. Firmware reported about
11.14 V near the end, consistent with the meter (its voltage is filtered).
Thus the voltage drop is real, not solely an ADC artifact. Battery-output
measurement is needed to separate battery/BMS sag from downstream wiring,
switch, connector or distribution losses. Per-driver VMot was not accessible.
Keep the initial current profile until this is investigated.

These are mechanically unloaded tests. Loaded grass torque, sustained thermal
performance, hardware VMot-loss recovery, and radio-loaded control timing have
not been established. Reset/fault rejection was simulated on the host, not
provoked electrically on the rack. WiFi was not configured; the extended
WebSocket diagnostics were compiled but not exercised over the network.

## Later connection repair and controller replacement

After fitting crimp terminals (still 22 AWG), the owner measured a 13.08 V
minimum during the same ten-second rack spin, versus 11.2 V previously.
Firmware sampled 12.91 V during motion; no faults were reported. This supports
connection resistance contributing to the earlier loss, without establishing
loaded grass performance.

The original XIAO later stopped enumerating, including when tested separately
by the owner. A replacement ESP32-S3 (MAC 64:e8:33:51:2a:70, 8 MB flash) was
installed with the full 0.1.5 build and existing 4 MB partition layout. Factory
flash was backed up to /tmp/ubot-replacement-factory-backup.bin before erasure.
Recorded gains 1.01616/1.00971, shaft polarity 0/1, SpreadCycle, 1200/600 mA
requested currents, and batt_div 11.126 were restored via an NVS image.
Battery scaling should be checked against the meter on the replacement ADC.
Wi-Fi credentials were unavailable and need provisioning again.

USB console, both encoders, BLE discovery/status, and both TMC UART status reads
worked. Enabling/disabling verified both driver configurations without a motion
command; no faults or echo errors were reported, and both drivers were left
disabled. The all-session worst control interval reached 102843 us during the
enable/status check; this is not a steady-motion timing measurement. No wheel
spin or live Wi-Fi validation was performed on the replacement in this check.

## Replacement Wi-Fi investigation (0.1.6)

After provisioning, the replacement joined Sam@Home2G at 192.168.1.157 with
RSSI around -51 to -55 dBm. The owner confirmed BLE operation and an attached
external antenna. The Mac resolved ubot.local to the correct new IP at least
once; direct-IP HTTP also initially returned 200. Subsequent direct-IP pings
and HTTP timed out, so name resolution alone does not explain the failure.
The Mac's ARP entry matched the replacement MAC. One service TXT lookup still
showed an older firmware version, consistent with stale discovery information.
A Wi-Fi reconnect logged reason 15 (four-way handshake timeout).

Built/flashed 0.1.6, preserving NVS, to add runtime `wifi ps on|off` and default
to modem sleep off. Build/hash verification passed; drivers remained disabled.
After reboot, HTTP returned 200 once and 5/8 direct-IP pings succeeded, with
67–511 ms RTT; a later 12-second WebSocket telemetry attempt timed out. Thus
modem sleep off has NOT established a reliable link or resolved the underlying
problem. Physical-phone direct-IP testing remains the next isolation step.
